TL;DR

What you need to know

Of 150 items collected this week, 88 (58.7%) were flagged Negative by the OCV ingestion pipeline. The three biggest negative-feedback topics are Action not executed (Topic 1, 29.5%) - mostly drafting, scheduling, and mailbox actions that Copilot claimed to perform but didn't - Reliability failure (Topic 6, 13.6%) - canned error strings, hangs, and blank responses, and Wrong context / grounding (Topic 8, 11.4%) - replies sent to the wrong thread, lost prior-turn context, wrong file scanned. Together these three topics account for 54.5% of all negative feedback this week.

Required caveats (do not omit):

  • Sentiment is not satisfaction. The 58.7% Negative share reflects the OCV pipeline's sentiment-filtered ingestion, not a population-level dissatisfaction rate.
  • Sampling bias. OCV feedback is self-selected and skews toward dissatisfied users.
  • Small-sample topics. Topics with n<3 this week (Topics 9, 11, 13) are anecdotal and should not be quoted as trends.
  • WoW basis is now methodologically consistent. Prior week (May 4-9) was re-classified with the same model + 13-topic taxonomy + priority rules. WoW deltas below are directly comparable, not migrated from a heuristic.

Key findings

What the data is telling us

Action execution is the single largest pain point (29.5%, n=26)

Across drafting, scheduling, mailbox, rules, and triage tools, users consistently report that Copilot claims success - 'I've sent the email', 'Your 5 drafts are in Drafts', 'Inbox rule created' - but the actual state has not changed. This is a trust-eroding pattern because it forces users to verify every action.

Reliability failure rebounds toward error-string mode

Topic 6 is 13.6% this week. Of the 12 reliability hits, 7 are canned error strings, 4 hangs, and 1 blank. The mix has shifted away from the blank-output pattern that dominated last week.

Wrong context / grounding (Topic 8) is 11.4% this week vs 6.5% last week

(▲4.9pp). Users report Copilot replying to the wrong email, editing the wrong paragraph, losing context from prior conversation turns, and grounding search/summary results on the wrong source.

Output-intent miss (Topic 3) is 9.1% vs 14.6% last week

Moderately down WoW.

File I/O issues, especially attachment ingestion

, remain a steady pain at 10.2% (Topic 10). The input SubMode (n=6) outweighs output (n=3) - users want screenshots, PDFs, Word redlines, and attached emails treated as first-class context.

Inaccurate / fabricated content (Topic 7) at 10.2%

clusters in Search and Summarization - hallucinated read/unread counts, incorrect day-of-week mappings, false 'reply already sent' claims, missing items in search results.

HitL violations (Topic 2)

are low-volume (n=4) but high-severity: emails being sent or archived without an undo path. Anecdotal at this n but worth tracking.

Calendar-correctness (Topic 11, n=1) and Intrusive UI (Topic 13, n=1) are anecdotal

at this sample size.
