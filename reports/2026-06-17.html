TL;DR

What you need to know

  • Volume: 213 verbatim items were ingested for the window; 125 (58.7%) carried negative sentiment and were classified into the 13-topic taxonomy.
  • Top failure mode: Action not executed (Topic 1, 34 items / 27.2%) — drafts, calendar invites, and rules reported as done or never inserted into Outlook. 13 of these are false-success claims (P0).
  • Close runner-up: Wrong context / grounding (Topic 8, 33 items / 26.4%) surged ▲15.1pp WoW, driven almost entirely by auto-rewrite/refine not receiving the user's selected or open text.
  • Concentration: Topics 1 and 8 together account for 53.6% of all negatives; the Drafting surface alone holds 43 items (34.4%, ▲16.9pp WoW).
  • Improvements: Inaccurate (Topic 7 ▼11.8pp) and Capability refusal (Topic 12 ▼8.1pp) both receded WoW.
  • Recurring quality bug: random foreign-language text (Arabic/Estonian) injected into otherwise-English output recurs again this week (Topic 9, 4 items).
  • Caveats: OCV feedback is self-selected and skews toward dissatisfied users (<1% of MAU); any topic with n < 3 (this week: Topics 5, 10, 11, 13) is anecdotal; the negative share reflects sentiment-filtered ingestion, not overall satisfaction; WoW deltas are approximate because the prior week's pull used --include-structured (1302 items) while this week is verbatim-only (213).

Key findings

What the data is telling us

Action-completion failures dominate (Topic 1, 27.2%)
P0

Across Drafting, Scheduling, Rules, and Triage, users asked Copilot to perform an action and it either did nothing or reported success without effect. 13 items are false-success claims ("meeting scheduled", "60 invites removed", "rule created", "emails deleted") — a trust-integrity class flagged P0.

The "Edit-and-Send" / compose hand-off is the single most cited Drafting defect

Multiple users report the draft renders in the side panel but never opens or inserts into an actual Outlook email, forcing copy/paste.

Grounding regressed sharply (Topic 8 ▲15.1pp)

Auto-rewrite/refine repeatedly does not receive the user's selected/highlighted text or the open message body; it rewrites the received email, an earlier draft, or produces generic content. This is the clearest cross-user, reproducible signal this week (21 Drafting items).

Reliability errors concentrate on informational surfaces (Topic 6)

Generic "I wasn't able to process your request" responses cluster on inbox summaries and needs-reply triage — the exact prompts Copilot itself recommends.

Inaccuracy eased but persists (Topic 7 ▼11.8pp)

Remaining cases include fabricated details, wrong HasAttachments, a decline miscategorized as an accomplishment, and inaccurate settings/how-to directions citing non-existent UI.

Foreign-language injection recurs (Topic 9)

Random Arabic/Estonian text appears in invite confirmations and summaries — a recurring output-quality defect worth a guardrail.

Surface shift:

feedback moved from Search (▼14.5pp WoW) toward Drafting (▲16.9pp), consistent with the Topic 1/8 concentration on compose and rewrite.

Anecdotal (n=1): file-output-as-xlsx (Topic 10), attendee-availability check (Topic 11), and an intrusive editable-draft widget in Catalyst (Topic 13) are single instances and not yet statistically meaningful.
