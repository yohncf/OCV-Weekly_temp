TL;DR

What you need to know

  • Volume: 1,193 feedback submissions this week; 132 (11%) left a written comment, 1,061 (89%) were no-comment thumbs.
  • Overall rating (whole-population): 935 thumbs-up (78%) vs. 258 thumbs-down (22%) - most users who rated did not comment and were positive.
  • Verbatim sentiment (commenters): 75 Negative (57%), 29 Neutral (22%), 28 Positive (21%). The 75 Negative items are the classified set.
  • Top topic: Wrong context / grounding (Topic 8) accounts for 34 of 75 negatives (45%), up ~16pp WoW - the dominant theme this week.
  • Runner-ups: Tone/language/format (Topic 9, 10 items, 13%) and a three-way tie of Action-not-executed, Inaccurate content, and Reliability at the mid-single digits.
  • WoW shifts: Reliability failures (Topic 6) fell sharply (~-11pp) and Action-not-executed (Topic 1) fell (~-9pp), while grounding/retrieval (Topic 8) and format (Topic 9) rose.
  • Caveats: OCV feedback is self-selected and skews toward dissatisfied users (verbatim < 1% of MAU); the negative share reflects sentiment-filtered ingestion, not user satisfaction; any topic with n < 3 is anecdotal; WoW deltas use percentage-of-negatives (prior denominator 93 vs. 75 this week).

Key findings

What the data is telling us

Grounding/retrieval is the week's dominant pain point
P1

34 of 75 negatives (45%) are Topic 8 - the agent cannot find, retrieve, or correctly scope mailbox content. The largest sub-cluster is search that returns no results for emails the user says exist (multiple P1 rows).

A specific, reproducible sub-pattern:

existing Outlook folders are reported as missing or "not in the accessible folder list" (2 items), and folder extractions are truncated to a fraction of matching messages (3 items, same NB-Submitted workflow).

False-success on a mailbox action
P0

(Topic 1): one item claims a folder-move completed while the mailbox did not change - flagged P0 for trust impact despite n=1.

Format/actionability rose

(Topic 9, 10 items): search results rendered as non-clickable text/grids, unwanted emojis, and one case of garbled non-English characters in output.

Capability handling is mixed

(Topic 12, 5 items): most are correct out-of-scope refusals, but at least one in-scope drafting request was refused with the capability statement (a misfire engineering can address).

Positive movement:

Reliability failures (canned errors/blanks) and action-not-executed both declined WoW, suggesting fewer hard system failures even as grounding complaints grew.

Findings resting on n < 3 (e.g., garbled-language output, lost multi-turn context, free/busy retrieval) are anecdotal and noted as such.
