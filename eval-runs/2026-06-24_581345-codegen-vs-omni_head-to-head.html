Read the headline with care: 32 of OMNI’s 40 losses are empty replies

32 of the 40 assertions where CodeGen wins are scored against a literally empty OMNI response (concentrated in Calendar OOO + Rules), plus 16 more rows where one arm returned N/A. These are completion / capture failures on heavy compound tasks, not quality regressions. CodeGen’s reply is never empty in its 47 losses — so OMNI’s 47 wins are all genuine. Net quality on captured replies favors OMNI more than +2.0pp implies.

CodeGen better OMNI replied empty or “no mail found” on rule-creation and rule-suggestion prompts; CodeGen confirmed the created rule with its condition and action.
CodeGen better OMNI failed to confirm the rule + folder action; CodeGen completed and summarized it.

OMNI better OMNI confirmed pin/flag actions explicitly; CodeGen under-reported the action taken.
OMNI better OMNI enumerated unread items more completely (subject/sender/count) than CodeGen.
both fail Folder deletion is unsupported for both — CodeGen reports no available tool; OMNI reports the folder “could not be found.” Capability gap, not a model regression.
