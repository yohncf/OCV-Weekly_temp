TL;DR

What you need to know

  • Overall rating (all feedback): 76.3% thumbs-up (1,058 up / 329 down of 1,387 total submissions), down ~2.8pp from 79.1% last week. This is the whole-population satisfaction signal.
  • Volume classified: 148 of the 1,387 submissions left a written comment (verbatim); of those, 93 (62.8%) are Negative and were classified into the taxonomy below. The remaining 1,239 are no-comment thumbs (1,020 up / 219 down).
  • Top failure mode: Wrong context / grounding (Topic 8) leads at 27 items (29.0%), slightly up from 26.4% last week - dominated by the Rewrite-feature not receiving selected text and reply-drafting on failed mailbox retrieval.
  • Reliability surged: Reliability failures (Topic 6) nearly doubled to 16 items (17.2%) from 9.6%, driven by recurring 'unable to retrieve your data / token expired / connection issue' errors on search and triage.
  • Action-not-executed eased: Topic 1 fell to 16 items (17.2%) from 27.2%, though it still includes P0 false-success claims (draft/OOF/calendar reported done but not performed).
  • Trust signals (P0): HitL review-surface gaps (Topic 2) reappeared at 2 items, and false-success claims persist - both filed P0.
  • Surfaces: Drafting (28) and Search (18) account for ~49% of negatives; the 'only top-level folders visible' limitation recurs across Rules/Triage.

Required caveats: The rating (76.3% up) is whole-population (all 1,387 submissions). All topic/subtopic analysis below is verbatim-only (the 148 who wrote a comment, 93 of them Negative) - written feedback is self-selected and skews toward dissatisfied users, so the verbatim sentiment split (63% Negative) must NOT be read as overall satisfaction. Any topic/subtopic with n < 3 is anecdotal; WoW deltas are approximate because the prior window was 10 days vs 8 this week.

Key findings

What the data is telling us

Grounding is the largest bucket (27/93)
P1

The recurring pattern is the Rewrite/edit action not receiving the user's selected email text (5 items, P1) and reply-drafting falling back to confident but ungrounded generic content when mailbox retrieval fails (5 items, P1).

Retrieval reliability degraded week-over-week

14 of 16 Topic 6 items are 'unable to retrieve your data / token expired / connection issue' surfaced on search and triage; multiple users describe it as frequent and tenant-wide. This is the clearest WoW regression (+7.6pp).

P0 trust breaks persist but are low-volume
P0

False-success claims (action reported done but not performed) span drafting, automatic-replies, and calendar (3 items), and review-surface gaps where a draft/invite was not shown inline before commit (2 items).

Folder visibility limits mailbox actions

Rule creation and move/triage actions fail when the target is a nested (non-top-level) folder - flagged explicitly by users (anecdotal but consistent across 4 items spanning Rules/Triage).

Calendar/rule automation refusals may be miscalibrated

4 items show the agent refusing in-scope automation (auto-accept, delete/create instructions), in one case a prompt Copilot itself suggested. Needs scope confirmation.

Output-quality defects recur:

stray Hindi/Devanagari characters in English replies (2 items) and non-clickable/broken email links (2 items) are repeat offenders.

Anecdotal (n<3):

wrong 'today' date in a planner, raw-metadata dumps, and external-contact fabrication are single/low-count signals to watch, not yet systemic.
