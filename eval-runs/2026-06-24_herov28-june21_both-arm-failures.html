The verdict — three buckets

What actually caused the failures

Only ~8% were genuinely bad assertions. The real signal: nearly a quarter failed because the test mailbox simply had nothing to act on, and the large majority were the agent itself — refusing, erroring, or skipping required confirmation steps.

The headline pattern

It's mostly HITL — confirmation gates and capability gaps

The single largest failure mode (32 of 78) is the agent performing or skipping human-in-the-loop steps incorrectly. On destructive actions it acted without confirming first — deleting a rule, a task list, and a contact; bulk-deleting 15 emails; creating an auto-delete rule. A further 10 were flat capability refusals (“I don't have a tool to delete/move/rename folders”).
